Support

Overview

Japan Travel Money is designed to minimize data collection. All settings and preferences are stored locally on your device. The app does not require account creation or login.

Data we collect

  • We do not collect personal data for advertising.
  • Your settings (language, currency, tax mode) are stored locally on your device only.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not require or store any account information.

Exchange Rate API

  • The app fetches live exchange rates from a third-party API to provide up-to-date currency conversion.
  • This request is made from your device and does not include any personal information.

Purchases

  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store in-app purchase).
  • We use RevenueCat to manage purchase status.
  • RevenueCat may process an anonymous user ID to verify purchase entitlements.

Third-party services

  • RevenueCat (purchase management): Privacy Policy

Data retention

All data is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

Children's privacy

Japan Travel Money does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
