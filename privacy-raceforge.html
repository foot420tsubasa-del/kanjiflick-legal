Privacy Policy for RaceForge

Last updated: March 14, 2026

RaceForge ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the RaceForge mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: such as email address or sign-in identifiers, if account creation is supported.
  • Workout and Training Data: such as completed workouts, logs, progress, performance history, goals, or preferences you enter in the App.
  • Device Information: such as device type, operating system, app version, language, and basic diagnostic data.
  • Purchase Information: subscription status and transaction metadata provided by Apple or third-party subscription tools. We do not receive your full payment card information.
  • Analytics Data: information about how users interact with the App, such as screens viewed, feature usage, crashes, and general performance metrics.

2. How We Use Information

We use information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the App
  • Save your training progress and preferences
  • Improve app performance, features, and user experience
  • Respond to support requests
  • Manage subscriptions and access to premium features
  • Monitor bugs, crashes, and abuse

3. Health and Fitness Data

RaceForge may allow users to record fitness-related information such as workout activity, effort, goals, or training performance. This information is used only to provide app functionality and improve the user experience. We do not sell personal health or fitness data to third parties.

4. Payments and Subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription, payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. We may use a third-party service such as RevenueCat to validate subscription status and manage access to premium content. We do not directly collect or store your credit card or payment details.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services to operate the App, such as:

  • Apple App Store / Apple In-App Purchase
  • RevenueCat for subscription management
  • Analytics and crash reporting providers

6. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide the App, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. If account deletion is supported, you may request deletion of your data.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

RaceForge is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or request a copy of your personal information. To make such a request, please contact us using the information below.
