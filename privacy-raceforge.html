Overview

RaceForge is designed to minimize data collection. All diagnostic data is stored locally on your device.

Data we collect

  • We do not collect personal data for advertising.
  • Your diagnostic results, settings, and checklist data are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not track your location.

Purchases

  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple (in-app purchase).
  • We use RevenueCat to manage purchase status.
  • RevenueCat may process an anonymous user ID to verify purchase entitlements.

Third-party services

  • RevenueCat (purchase management): Privacy Policy

Data retention

All data is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes all data.

Children's privacy

RaceForge does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
